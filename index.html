    🛡️ Risk Management & Rules

    Strategy decides when to trade. Risk management decides whether you are still trading next year.

    Hard Rules

    • Risk 0.5–1% of the account per trade. Never more than 2%.
    • Maximum 4 trades per day. Max daily loss 3% → stop for the day.
    • Stop after 2–3 consecutive losses; review instead of revenge trading.
    • Every trade has a stop-loss before entry. Never widen it.
    • Minimum 1:1.5 R:R unless the strategy has a proven high win rate.
    • Move SL to breakeven only after price reaches ~1R or a new structure forms.
    • No new trades 15–30 min before/after high-impact (red-folder) news.
    • Correlated pairs (EURUSD + GBPUSD) = the same trade; count the risk together.

    Win Rate Needed to Break Even

    R:RBreak-even win %Expectancy @ 50% win

    Expectancy (in R) = Win% × R − Loss% × 1. Positive = profitable over many trades.

    Psychology & Process

    • Trade your plan, not your P&L. Hide the account balance if it distracts you.
    • No setup = no trade. Sitting out is a position.
    • Journal every trade: screenshot, reason, emotion, result, R multiple.
    • FOMO after a missed move → wait for the next pullback instead.
    • Judge yourself on rule-following, not single trade outcomes.
    • Review weekly: which setups pay you? Trade more of those, cut the rest.
